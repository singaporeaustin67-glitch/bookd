    Straight answers

    The fine print, on the table.

    Where does the contact data come from?

    Public business registries, trade and procurement signals, and licensed sources — processed under GDPR / CCPA. Records live in the vault only; they're never resold or exposed.

    Won't AI emails look like spam?

    Every draft is personalized from that buyer's context and must clear a quality score before it ships. We sell meetings, not mail volume — anything below threshold never leaves the outbox.

    Who decides what counts as a qualified meeting?

    You do. At kickoff you set the ICP rules (industry, seniority, region, buying signals). Meetings that don't match the rules aren't billable.

    What do I need to plug in?

    Google or Outlook calendar, plus HubSpot or Salesforce if you use them. Synced once, meetings and statuses update in your pipeline automatically.

    Can I restrict geography or industry?

    Yes. Region, language, industry, and company size are all filterable; markets you don't serve go on an exclusion list.
